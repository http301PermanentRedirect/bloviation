Anyone who says "it can't rain all the time" has never lived in Titan's Stairs. In The Stairs there are only two types of weather: raining, or going to rain. It rains two days out of every three and it's a wonder the people who live there don't grow gills or scales. Well, then again, some of them do. The entire city is made of stone, carved into the rocks and rubble from the long-quiecent volcano that stands above the city like a great smoking chimney. Wood, brick, or other building materials rots or melts away; even the stone must eventually be replaced and repaired.
